# Privacy Policy for Kirjakaveri

*Last updated: September 21, 2026*

**Kirjakaveri** ("we", "our", or "us") provides a library discovery and reading tracker application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We only collect information necessary to provide and improve the application:
- **Account Information:** When you choose to sign in (via Google or Email), we collect your name and email address to manage your user profile and authenticate your access.
- **User-Authored Content:** Books you save to your wishlist, reading lists, personal notes, ratings, and reading goals are stored in your private cloud account via Google Firebase Firestore.
- **Location Information:** If you grant location permission, we use your device's approximate GPS coordinates solely on-device to calculate distances to nearby public library branches. We do not track, log, or store your location on our servers.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** If you enable reading reminders, a notification token is stored locally on your device.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services and APIs
Kirjakaveri utilizes public APIs and standard infrastructure services:
- **Finna.fi API (National Library of Finland):** Used to search bibliographic records and book metadata.
- **Kirjastot.fi API:** Used to display public library branch details, coordinates, and opening schedules.
- **Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore):** Used for secure user sign-in and encrypted cloud storage of your reading lists.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information
- To provide, personalize, and synchronize your reading logs across your devices.
- To find and display public library branches near you.
- To deliver optional reading reminders and notifications that you have configured.

---

### 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Data is only processed through secure infrastructure providers (Google Firebase) strictly for core app functionality.

---

### 5. Data Security & Encryption
All data transmitted between the Kirjakaveri app and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS). Stored user data in Firebase is protected by strict access control rules.

---

### 6. Data Deletion and Account Control
You have full control over your data. You can delete individual books, logs, or your entire account directly from within the app settings. You may also contact us at any time to request complete erasure of your data.

---

### 7. Children's Privacy
Kirjakaveri is a general audience educational and utility application and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.
